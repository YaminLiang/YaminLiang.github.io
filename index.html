How I work

Order first

Sort by who decides, not by function

Clinical systems are not feature collections. They are sequences of judgement. I map the sequence before drawing a single screen.

One state model

Thirty products, one grammar

States, permissions and exception paths should be shared across product lines, or every team invents its own version of “abnormal”.

Willing to subtract

Density is not honesty

Showing everything is a way to avoid deciding. I would rather cut a field than let a clinician guess what it means.

If you are building something in medical, care or robotics — I would like to hear about it.
